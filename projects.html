Magic Chess.

[ Placeholder: System Architecture Diagram ]

The Objective

Traditional digital chessboards rely on physical sensors under every square. The goal was to build a zero-sensor system relying purely on Edge AI vision and asynchronous kinematics for physical-to-digital state synchronization.

Core Stack

  • > Vision: YOLO11n, OpenCV
  • > Backend: Python, FastAPI
  • > Engine: Stockfish 16.1
  • > Hardware: Arduino, Serial IO

Vision Confidence Algorithm

To prevent the robotic arm from executing erroneous moves due to lighting shifts, the vision pipeline utilizes Intersection over Union (IoU) to validate piece bounding boxes before dispatching serial commands.

$$ \text{IoU} = \frac{\text{Area of Overlap}}{\text{Area of Union}} = \frac{|A \cap B|}{|A \cup B|} $$
PERFORMANCE_METRICS:
> Vision Accuracy: 94.2 mAP50
> Continuous Frame Rate: 60 FPS
> Logic Execution Latency: Sub-50 ms

Modernization Architecture

AFIDIpro.

Legacy Bottleneck

The platform originally ran on a tightly coupled TypeScript monolith. As user traffic scaled, it suffered from severe latency spikes during heavy read-writes and lacked robust security boundaries between user cohorts.

The Migration Strategy

Led the architectural shift to a decoupled Progressive Monolith. By migrating the backend entirely to Python and FastAPI, I separated the frontend delivery from backend logic.

  • > Decoupled routing from business logic.
  • > Migrated data layer to highly-indexed PostgreSQL.
  • > Hardened access controls and session management to OWASP standards.
